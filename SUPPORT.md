## Asking Questions & Reporting Bugs

- **General questions & ideas**: Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Ceph-learner/ListenScope/discussions) (Q&A / Ideas / Show & tell).
- **Bugs**: Open an issue via the [Bug Report template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.yml) and attach your diagnostic bundle, version (footer), OS, GPU, and capture mode.
- **Security vulnerabilities**: Please report privately via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than a public issue.
